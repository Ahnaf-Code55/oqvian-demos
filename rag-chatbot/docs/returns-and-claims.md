# Returns and claims

Ask your Kestrel account contact for a return reference before sending freight back. Include the original shipment reference, the reason for return, the number of pieces, and the return address. The receiving business must confirm that it can accept the goods. Kestrel will provide a return label or routing instructions after the route and service are confirmed.

For visible damage or a missing piece, note the condition on the carrier receipt before signing when possible. Photograph the outside packaging, the shipping label, and the goods. Keep the packaging until the claim is reviewed. Report loss or damage within 5 calendar days of delivery and include the shipment reference, photos, a short description, and an invoice or other value record.

Kestrel acknowledges a complete claim within 2 business days. This is an acknowledgement window, not a promise that the claim will be settled in that time. The carrier or insurer may request an inspection or further evidence before making a decision.

Transport charges are not automatically refunded when a claim is opened. Return transport, inspection fees, and disposal are separate services and are quoted before arrangement. Do not discard affected goods until Kestrel confirms that an inspection is no longer required.
