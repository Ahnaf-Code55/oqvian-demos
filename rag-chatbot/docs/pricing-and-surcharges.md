# Pricing and surcharges

Every Kestrel Freight quote is based on the route, service level, shipment dimensions, actual weight, pickup conditions, and delivery access. Send the origin and destination, number of pieces, length, width, height, gross weight, pickup date, and any special handling request. Quotes are valid for 14 calendar days unless the quote itself lists a shorter period.

The billable weight is the greater of actual weight and dimensional weight. For this sample service, dimensional weight in kilograms is length times width times height in centimetres divided by 5,000 for each piece. Add the piece values together. For example, a 50 by 40 by 30 centimetre carton has a dimensional weight of 12 kilograms.

A remote-area delivery may add a 24 unit surcharge per shipment. A requested pickup outside the normal weekday window may add a 35 unit after-hours fee. These example charges are in the fictional service's billing units and are separate from transport charges, duties, and taxes. Any applicable surcharge should appear as its own line on the written quote.

Kestrel confirms the final billable weight after measurement. If the measured dimensions differ from the information supplied, the invoice may change. Ask for a revised written quote before booking when shipment details change.
