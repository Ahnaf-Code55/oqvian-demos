# Holiday schedule

Kestrel Freight's fictional operations desk is open Monday through Friday, 08:30 to 17:00 local time. The desk is closed on Saturdays, Sundays, and the dates listed below. Times are local to the relevant origin terminal. Carrier and customs authority calendars may differ from Kestrel's desk calendar.

The sample closure dates for 2026 are 1 January, 16 February, 1 May, 31 August, and 25 December. The desk reopens at 08:30 on the next business day after each closure. A booking request received while the desk is closed is reviewed when the desk reopens, and the pickup date is not reserved until a booking reference is issued.

For a planned holiday shipment, send the route, piece count, dimensions, weight, and preferred pickup date at least 5 business days before the closure. Kestrel will check carrier availability and tell you whether the usual service window still applies. A terminal closure or customs holiday can add time even when the Kestrel desk is open.

For an existing shipment, use its reference number in a status request. Urgent requests received outside desk hours are handled on the next open day. Kestrel does not promise a weekend or holiday response for this sample service.
