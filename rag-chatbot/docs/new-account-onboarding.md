# Onboarding a new account

To open a Kestrel Freight business account, provide the registered business name, billing address, primary operations contact, invoice contact, and the routes you expect to use. The onboarding team sends a short account form and asks for one recent business registration document. Do not send passwords, payment card numbers, or identity documents by ordinary email.

After the form is complete, Kestrel reviews it within 3 business days. The team then confirms the account reference, billing terms, and the named contacts who may request pickups. New accounts begin with invoice terms of 14 days from invoice date unless a signed agreement lists different terms. A credit review may be needed before a credit limit is confirmed.

Before the first shipment, share an accurate pickup address, access hours, package dimensions, gross weights, and a contact who can meet the driver. Kestrel checks the first booking for missing fields and returns it for correction if needed. A shipment is not confirmed until a booking reference is issued.

Account changes should be requested by an existing named contact. Include the account reference and the fields to change. Kestrel confirms changes by replying to the registered contact address.
