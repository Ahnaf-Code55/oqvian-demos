# Data and security FAQ

Kestrel Freight uses the contact details, addresses, shipment descriptions, and tracking references supplied by customers to arrange and support freight services. Information may be shared with the carrier, terminal, customs broker, or delivery partner that needs it to handle a shipment. Kestrel does not need an account password to prepare a quote or trace a shipment.

Send routine shipment documents through the account portal or to the registered account contact. For a suspected privacy or security issue, contact the account team and include the time, affected shipment reference if known, and a way to reach you. Do not include passwords or full payment details in the report.

Access to account records is limited to staff who need the information for their work. Customers should tell Kestrel when an authorized contact leaves or changes role so access can be updated. A request to correct contact or address information should come from a named account contact and include the account reference.

This fictional service's sample retention schedule keeps routine shipment records for 24 months after delivery, then removes or de-identifies them unless a legal, billing, or open-claim need requires a longer period. Requests about a specific record can be sent to the account contact for review.
