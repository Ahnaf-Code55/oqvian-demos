# Shipping times

Kestrel Freight coordinates small-business shipments between the fictional ports of Alder Bay, North Quay, and Bellhaven. The estimates below count business days after cargo is accepted at the origin terminal. They are planning windows, not guaranteed delivery dates.

For Alder Bay to North Quay, allow 2 to 3 business days by standard freight service. Alder Bay to Bellhaven usually takes 5 to 7 business days. A North Quay to Bellhaven shipment takes 3 to 4 business days. A shipment that includes a customs inspection can take 1 to 2 additional business days after the inspection begins.

To request a time estimate, send the origin, destination, package count, approximate weight, and preferred pickup date to your account contact. Kestrel confirms the service window after checking available carrier space. The clock starts when the carrier records the freight as accepted, not when a quote is requested or a pickup is booked.

Weather, terminal congestion, incomplete paperwork, and missed collection windows may change the estimate. Customers should keep the shipment reference number and check for status updates before arranging labor at the destination.
